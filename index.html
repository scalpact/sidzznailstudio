Questions before you book

How do I book an appointment?

Send a message on Instagram with the design you like and a day that suits you. You will get a confirmed time slot in reply.

How long do gel nails last?

With normal care, gel polish and extensions usually stay fresh for two to three weeks. Refills keep extensions looking new.

Do you ship press-on nails outside Surat?

Yes. Custom press-on sets are made to your size and shipped anywhere in India.

How do I pay for press-ons?

Press-ons are made to order, so they need full online payment in advance. There is no cash on delivery, return or exchange on custom sets.

Do you teach nail art?

Yes. The nail art course runs for 10 days, 2 hours a day, with a certificate. All practice material is provided.

What are the studio timings?

Monday to Saturday 10 am to 8 pm, Sunday 10 am to 6 pm, by appointment.
